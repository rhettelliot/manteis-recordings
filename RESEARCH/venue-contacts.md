## Booking Approach

1. **Start with Barboza** — best size and genre fit for Manteis sound
2. **Ecstatic Dance Seattle** — natural audience match for deep textural electronic
3. **KEXP radio play** — submit music first, then leverage for venue booking
4. **Gallery spaces** — ambient sets pair well with art exhibitions
5. **Kremwerk** — for underground techno/DnB nights

## Key Notes

- Most Seattle venues book 2-3 months out
- Electronic music scene is strongest Oct-April (rainy season drives indoor events)
- Summer = outdoor festivals and fewer club nights
- KEXP airplay significantly boosts booking chances
- Bring your own promoter/door split for smaller venues
- Sound system quality varies — ask about specs before booking
