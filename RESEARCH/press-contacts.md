## Approach Strategy

1. **KEXP airplay first** — submit music, get on local radio, leverage for everything else
2. **The Stranger** — pitch as local Seattle electronic label. Contact music writers directly.
3. **Resident Advisor** — pitch the warehouse/DnB/techno sound. Best global press target.
4. **Bandcamp Daily** — set up Bandcamp store first, then pitch for editorial feature
5. **Reddit communities** — engage genuinely, share in appropriate threads, build reputation
6. **Spotify for Artists** — submit unreleased tracks for editorial playlist consideration
7. **Pitchfork** — long shot, but the Greek etymology and textural approach is a unique angle

## Key Notes

- Always have a professional one-sheet ready (bio, photo, streaming links, catalog)
- Press pitches should reference a specific release, not "check out our music"
- Build relationships before asking for coverage — engage with writers on social media
- Exclusives matter — offer premieres to blogs/publications
- Timing: pitch 4-6 weeks before a release for pre-coverage
- Follow up once, never twice — respect their inbox
- KEXP airplay is the strongest single signal for Seattle music press
