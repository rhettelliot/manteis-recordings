## 3. Algorithmic Playlists (automatic, no pitch needed)

### How to Trigger Algorithmic Placement
- **Release Radar** — automatic for followers of each artist profile. Grow followers.
- **Discover Weekly** — triggered by: listening behavior of existing listeners, save rates, playlist additions
- **Daily Mix** — appears for active listeners of an artist
- **AutoRap / Repeat** — triggered by repeat listening

### Tactics to Boost Algorithmic Placement
1. **Get saves, not just plays** — saves signal quality to the algorithm. Encourage saves in social posts.
2. **Get playlist additions** — when listeners add tracks to their own playlists, it signals relevance
3. **Cross-pollinate artists** — Thesan Musique listeners → Red Shift Mantra. The algorithm sees the label as an ecosystem.
4. **Consistent release cadence** — the algorithm favors active artists. Plan MR-010 for Q4 2026.
5. **Complete artist profiles** — bio, photos, canvas videos, artist pick — all signal professionalism to the algorithm
