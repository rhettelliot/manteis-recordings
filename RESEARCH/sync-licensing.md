# Sync Licensing Opportunities

> Research for Manteis Recordings — getting music placed in film, TV, games, ads.
> Last updated: 2026-08-12

## Sync Licensing Platforms

### 1. Musicbed
- **URL:** musicbed.com / artists.musicbed.com
- **Submission:** New Artist Submission Form (HubSpot-powered) via artists.musicbed.com
- **Acceptance rate:** Highly curated — "accepts less than 1% of artist submissions"
- **Model:** Single-song sync licenses + subscription pool payouts
- **Exclusivity:** Non-exclusive
- **Electronic fit:** Yes — they have electronic/ambient categories. Deep textural electronic could work.
- **Royalty rates:** 50/50 split on sync fees. Subscription pool pays per-stream. Payments via check/PayPal/ACH on the 15th for prior month.
- **Pros:** High-quality placements (film, TV, branded content). Good reputation. Not exclusive.
- **Cons:** Very competitive. Need polished, mastered tracks with clean metadata.
- **Action:** Submit 3-5 best tracks via the form. Include genre tags "electronic," "ambient," "cinematic."

### 2. Artlist
- **URL:** artlist.io
- **Submission:** Apply via artlist.io/pages/artlist-for-artists
- **Model:** Subscription-based — artists get a share of subscription revenue based on downloads
- **Exclusivity:** Exclusive on their platform (non-exclusive elsewhere for direct deals)
- **Electronic fit:** Yes — large electronic music catalog
- **Royalty rates:** Per-download share from subscription pool. No upfront sync fees.
- **Pros:** High volume of placements. Growing platform.
- **Cons:** Lower per-placement payout. Exclusivity requirement.
- **Action:** Apply with full catalog. Good for volume-based revenue.

### 3. Epidemic Sound
- **URL:** epidemicsound.com
- **Submission:** Apply via epidemicsound.com/for-artists/
- **Model:** Royalty-free library — artists get recurring payments from the royalty pool
- **Exclusivity:** Exclusive on platform
- **Electronic fit:** Yes — one of the largest electronic music catalogs
- **Royalty rates:** Share of subscription revenue based on streams/downloads. Direct sync deals possible. $4.2M annual Soundtrack Bonus pool.
- **Status:** Not currently accepting new artist applications as of Aug 2026 ("Stay tuned" message on site).
- **Pros:** Massive reach — used by YouTubers, streamers, TV. Consistent revenue. Upfront payment per track.
- **Cons:** Royalty-free model means lower per-placement value. Exclusive. Currently closed to new artists.
- **Action:** Good for passive revenue stream. Submit full catalog.

### 4. Marmoset
- **URL:** marmosetmusic.com
- **Submission:** Apply via marmosetmusic.com/music-licensing (artist submission)
- **Model:** Curated sync licensing — placements in film, TV, ads
- **Acceptance:** Curated. Portland-based, PNW focus.
- **Exclusivity:** Non-exclusive
- **Electronic fit:** Yes — they license electronic, ambient, cinematic music
- **Royalty rates:** 50/50 split on sync fees. Pro-sync deals.
- **Pros:** Portland/Seattle connection — PNW relationships. High-quality ad placements. Curated, not volume-based. Clients include Netflix, HBO, Apple, Amazon.
- **Cons:** Competitive. Need to fit their aesthetic.
- **Action:** **Best fit for Manteis.** Submit with pitch about Seattle/PNW connection. Send "Ataraxia" tracks.

### 5. Songtradr
- **URL:** songtradr.com
- **Submission:** Register at songtradr.com/user/register?type=artist
- **Model:** Marketplace + sync licensing platform. Artists upload, buyers search.
- **Exclusivity:** Non-exclusive
- **Electronic fit:** Yes — broad catalog
- **Royalty rates:** Artist sets own price. Platform takes commission.
- **Pros:** Direct marketplace model. More control over pricing. Global reach.
- **Cons:** Self-serve — less curation. Need to self-promote within platform.
- **Action:** Upload full catalog. Set competitive prices. Good supplementary platform.

## Strategy

1. **Marmoset first** — PNW connection, curated, non-exclusive, clients include Netflix/HBO/Apple/Amazon. Best cultural fit.
2. **Musicbed second** — high-quality placements, competitive but worth trying. Non-exclusive.
3. **Bungie direct outreach** — local, high-profile, genre fit.
4. **Songtradr** — marketplace, non-exclusive, more control. Skip Epidemic Sound for now (not accepting new artists).
5. **Netflix/HBO/Amazon** — long-shot direct outreach, but one placement = major revenue.

## Pricing Reference

- **Indie film sync:** $500 - $5,000 per placement
- **TV series sync:** $2,000 - $20,000 per placement
- **AAA game sync:** $5,000 - $50,000+ per placement
- **Ad campaign sync:** $10,000 - $100,000+ per placement
- **Platform royalties:** $0.01 - $0.50 per stream (varies by platform)

## Key Notes

- All tracks must be mastered and have clean metadata (BPM, key, mood tags)
- Instrumental versions are essential — most sync placements are instrumental
- Stems and alternate mixes increase placement likelihood
- ISRC codes required for all tracks
- Register with a PRO (ASCAP/BMI) for performance royalties on top of sync fees
- Build a "sync reel" — 5-10 tracks curated for different moods/uses
